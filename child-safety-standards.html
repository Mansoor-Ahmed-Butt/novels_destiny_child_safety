Child Safety Standards

App: Novels Destiny  |  Last updated: October 22, 2026

Novels Destiny has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). We are committed to keeping children safe and to preventing our app from being used to harm them in any way.

1. Prohibited Content and Behavior

The following is strictly forbidden in Novels Destiny, including in novels, chapters, comments, profiles, messages, images and any other user content:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form
  • Sexual or sexualized content involving minors (under 18)
  • Grooming, sexual solicitation or sextortion of minors
  • Sharing, promoting or requesting links to CSAM or CSAE content
  • Any content that encourages or glorifies the abuse or exploitation of children

2. Enforcement

Content or accounts that violate these standards are removed. Offending accounts are permanently banned. We do not give warnings for CSAE or CSAM violations.

3. In-App Reporting

Users can report content or users they believe put a child at risk directly inside the app. Every report related to child safety is reviewed with high priority, and violating content is removed as soon as it is confirmed.

4. Moderation and Prevention

  • User-generated content is reviewed when it is reported or flagged.
  • We act quickly to remove content that violates these standards.
  • We keep our community guidelines and moderation practices up to date.

5. Reporting to Authorities

We comply with all applicable child safety laws. When we become aware of CSAM or child exploitation, we remove it and report it to the relevant regional and national authorities and to organizations such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), and we cooperate with law enforcement investigations.
